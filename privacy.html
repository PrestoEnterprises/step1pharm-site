Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Step 1 Pharm is a pharmacology study app for medical students, made by Presto Enterprises LLC, a Georgia company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version
  • We collect what we need to run your account and track your studying.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Leaderboards are off until you turn them on.
  • You can turn off usage analytics and delete your account at any time in the app.

What we collect

WhatExamplesWhy
Account detailsEmail address, first and last name, a password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we never see it)To create and sign in to your account, and to send password-reset codes
ProfileDisplay name, avatar choice, medical school, graduation year, Step 1 exam date if you add oneTo personalize the app, set your daily study goal, and group you with your class

We don't collect: your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, advertising identifiers, or payment information. Study reminders are scheduled on your phone; we don't send them from a server.

Who can see what

  • Classmates (same school and graduation year) see your display name, avatar and weekly score on the class leaderboard, only if you turn on leaderboards in Settings → Privacy. It's off by default.
  • Friends you add see your display name, avatar, streak, cards mastered and whether you studied today.
  • Our team can see account and study data when needed to run the app, answer support requests, and review reported names or content.
  • Nobody else. Your email and real name are never shown to other students.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account and study data while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, study history, streaks, friends and leaderboard history are deleted right away. Copies in our database backups are removed as those backups expire, within 30 days.
  • Feedback messages you sent are deleted with your account. Questions you flagged, schools you added to the list, and invite records may be kept, with your account link removed.
  • Crash reports and analytics events are kept by those providers for a limited time (about 30 days for crash reports). After you delete your account, your analytics events stay under a random ID that no longer links to any account; email us and we'll delete them too.

Your choices

  • Usage analytics: Settings → Privacy → Share usage data.
  • Leaderboards: the leaderboard switch in Settings → Privacy.
  • Edit your profile: your name, school, avatar and exam date can be changed in Settings.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account, or see how to delete your account without the app.
  • Get a copy of your data or ask a question: email us (below). Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your data; we honor these requests for every user.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each student read and change only their own data, and quiz answers are graded on our servers. Your saved login on your phone is encrypted with a key kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Age

Step 1 Pharm is made for medical students and is not directed to children. You must be at least 18 to use it. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
