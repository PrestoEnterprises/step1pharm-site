1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Presto Enterprises LLC, a Georgia limited liability company ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses and otherwise processes personal information in connection with the Step 1 Pharm mobile application, related websites and any services we provide through them (collectively, the "Services").

By creating an account or otherwise using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Services. This Privacy Policy is incorporated into and subject to our Terms of Use.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Account information, such as your name, email address and password. If you sign in through a third-party account (for example, Apple or Google), we receive your name and email address from that provider as permitted by your settings with them.
  • Profile information, such as your display name, selected avatar, educational institution, graduation year and anticipated examination date.
  • Study and activity information, such as flashcard ratings, quiz responses and scores, study streaks, progress through course content, and virtual items earned within the Services.
  • Social information, such as connections you make with other users and your participation in leaderboards and group challenges.
  • Communications, such as feedback, content reports and support requests you send to us, and any information you choose to include in them.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system and version, and application version.
  • Usage information, such as the features and screens you use, the actions you take within the Services, and the dates and times of your activity.
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash reports, error logs and performance data.

We do not collect precise geolocation, contacts, photos, audio or advertising identifiers, and we do not use the Services to track you across third-party applications or websites. We do not process payment card information.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • provide, operate and maintain the Services, including your account and study progress;
  • personalize your study schedule, recommendations and goals;
  • provide social features such as leaderboards, group challenges and connections with other users;
  • communicate with you, including account, security and support messages;
  • monitor, troubleshoot and improve the performance, reliability and content of the Services;
  • analyze usage in de-identified or aggregated form to understand and improve the Services;
  • detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents and violations of our Terms of Use, including reviewing reported content; and
  • comply with applicable law and enforce our legal rights.

We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising.

4. How We Disclose Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose personal information only as described below:

  • Service providers. We engage vendors that perform services on our behalf, including cloud hosting and data storage, account authentication, email delivery, application diagnostics, product analytics and application distribution. These service providers may process personal information only on our instructions and are contractually required to protect it and to use it only to provide services to us.
  • Other users. Certain information is visible to other users as described in Section 5.
  • Legal requirements and protection. We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that doing so is necessary to comply with law, regulation, legal process or a governmental request; to enforce our Terms of Use; or to protect the rights, property or safety of the Company, our users or others.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.
  • With your consent. We may disclose information for other purposes with your consent or at your direction.

5. Information Visible to Other Users

  • Leaderboards are disabled by default. If you enable them, users at your educational institution with the same graduation year can see your display name, avatar and leaderboard results.
  • Connections you add can see your display name, avatar, study streak, progress summary and whether you have studied on the current day.

Your email address and legal name are not displayed to other users.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Account and profile. You may review and update your profile information at any time in the application's settings.
  • Leaderboards. You may enable or disable leaderboard visibility at any time in the application's privacy settings.
  • Usage analytics. You may opt out of usage analytics at any time in the application's privacy settings.
  • Notifications. You may enable or disable study reminders in the application or in your device settings.
  • Account deletion. You may delete your account at any time in the application's settings, or by following the instructions on our account deletion page.
  • Access, correction, deletion and portability. You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information by contacting us as described in Section 13. We will verify your request using the email address associated with your account and respond within the time required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify the personal information associated with it, except that we may retain:

  • content reports and similar submissions in a form no longer linked to your account, to maintain the quality of our content;
  • de-identified or aggregated information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you;
  • information in routine backups and diagnostic logs, which are deleted on a rolling basis, generally within 30 days; and
  • information we are required to keep to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes or enforce our agreements.

8. Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse and alteration, including encryption of data in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

9. Children's Privacy

The Services are intended for adults and are not directed to children. You must be at least 18 years old to use the Services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or a higher age where required by applicable law). If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

10. Location of Processing

The Company is based in the United States, and personal information is processed and stored in the United States. If you access the Services from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to, stored and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.
