Step 1 Pharm Support

Pharmacology flashcards, quizzes and a learning path for USMLE Step 1 study.

Contact us

Email support@prestoenterprise.com, or use Settings → Help → Send feedback in the app. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password". We'll email you a one-time code to type into the app, then you can choose a new password.

What counts toward my streak?

A day counts when you rate 10 flashcards or finish a quiz with at least 5 questions answered. Path quizzes, unit checks and the daily Class Challenge count as quizzes. A streak freeze from the shop covers a missed day automatically.

Who can see me on the leaderboard?

No one, until you turn leaderboards on in Settings → Privacy. Then classmates at your school and graduation year see your display name, avatar and weekly score. Friends you add see your streak and progress.

Something in a question or drug card looks wrong

Tap "Report a problem" on the question, or send feedback from Settings → Help. Our content team reviews every report.

Someone has an offensive name or is bothering me

Tap their name on the leaderboard or your friends list and choose Report. Our team reviews every report within 24 hours. You can also choose Block: they disappear from your leaderboards, stop being your friend, and aren't told. Manage blocked students in Settings → Privacy.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. If you no longer have the app, see delete your account.
