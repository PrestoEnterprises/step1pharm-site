Account Deletion

Step 1 Pharm · Presto Enterprises LLC

You may delete your Step 1 Pharm account and associated personal information at any time using either method below.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Step 1 Pharm and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2: By email

Send a request to support@prestoenterprise.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." We will verify the request and complete the deletion within 7 days, and confirm by email.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account credentials, email address, name and profile
  • Your study history, including flashcard, quiz, streak and progress data, and virtual items earned
  • Your connections, leaderboard results and challenge results
  • Feedback you submitted

Data that may be retained

  • Content reports you submitted, in a form no longer linked to your account
  • De-identified or aggregated information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you
  • Information in routine backups and diagnostic logs, which are deleted on a rolling basis, generally within 30 days
  • Information we are required to keep to comply with legal obligations

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
