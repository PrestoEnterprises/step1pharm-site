Delete your Step 1 Pharm account

Step 1 Pharm is made by Presto Enterprises LLC.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Step 1 Pharm and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email support@prestoenterprise.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Step 1 Pharm account". We'll confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email address, name and profile
  • All study history: flashcards, quiz answers, streaks, learning path progress, caps and items
  • Friends, leaderboard scores and class challenge results
  • Feedback messages you sent

What may be kept

  • Questions you flagged and schools you added to the list, with your account link removed, so we can keep improving the content
  • Usage analytics events, under a random ID that no longer links to any account. Email us and we'll delete those too
  • Database backups, which expire within 30 days
  • Crash reports, which our crash-reporting provider deletes automatically after about 30 days

See the Privacy Policy for details.
